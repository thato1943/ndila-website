A practical path from asset to sale

Auction or private treaty may be discussed, based on the asset and your goals. Ndila does not promise sale outcomes or prices.

    Tell Ndila what you are selling, where it is and what you hope to achieve.

  3. Plan preparation and marketing

    Agree the site preparation, advertising and marketing activity.

  4. Conduct the sale and follow up

    The support described can include the auction, final clean-up and consolidation.

Helpful information

Buyer and seller questions

General guidance only. Check the confirmed listing and sale terms for details about a specific auction.

For buyers

How do I take part in an auction?

Each confirmed listing will explain how to participate. The bidding platform is still pending owner confirmation, so no platform is represented as confirmed here.

Where can I find dates, inspection details and auction terms?

Review the information published with the confirmed listing. Inspection arrangements, dates and sale conditions can differ by auction and should be checked before bidding.

Are fees or deposits shown on this website?

No fee or deposit amounts are published here. Refer to the terms for the specific auction before making a bid.

For sellers

What can Ndila help sell?

The company profile describes fixed property and movable asset auctions, including vehicles, trucks, mining equipment and agricultural or farming-related assets. It also lists asset management and online auctions.

Can I discuss a private treaty sale?

The company profile describes both auction and private treaty as possible sale routes. Share the asset and your goals so the suitable approach can be discussed.

Does the website promise a sale price or outcome?

No. Any sale route and expected process should be discussed for the specific asset; the website does not promise a price or sale result.
